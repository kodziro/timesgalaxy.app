The Year 4 multiplication tables check: a times tables guide for parents

Last updated: 17 October 2026

The multiplication tables check (MTC) is a short online check that children in Year 4 in England take in June. It has 25 times tables questions up to 12 × 12, with 6 seconds for each answer. The best way to get ready is a few minutes of practice every day, so every fact comes quickly and surely, not a long session the week before.

What is the multiplication tables check?

The MTC is a statutory check for Year 4 pupils in England. Schools run it on a computer or tablet during a three-week window in June. It checks whether a child can recall the times tables fluently, which the national curriculum expects by the end of Year 4.

There is no pass mark. Each child gets a score out of 25, and the school uses it to see who needs more help.

How does the check work?

  • 25 questions, for example “7 × 8 = ?”.
  • 6 seconds to type each answer, then a 3-second pause before the next question.
  • The child types the answer with a keyboard or with the number pad on the screen.
  • 3 practice questions come first; they do not count.
  • The whole check takes less than 5 minutes.

Which times tables come up?

The times tables from 2 to 12. Questions from the 6, 7, 8, 9 and 12 times tables come up more often, because these are the hardest for most children. So it pays to spend the most practice time on facts like 6 × 8, 7 × 8, 8 × 9 and 12 × 7.

How fast does my child need to be?

Six seconds is enough for a child who knows the fact by heart. It is not enough to count up from zero or to work a hard fact out slowly. So the goal is recall: the answer comes in about 2–3 seconds, without counting.

Tricks are the bridge to get there. A trick for each table helps while a fact is new; practice on later days makes the answer automatic.

How can my child practise at home?

  1. 5 minutes a day. A short daily session works better than 30 minutes once a week.
  2. Easy tables first. ×1, ×2, ×10 and ×5 first; then ×3, ×4, ×6, ×9, ×8, ×7, ×11 and ×12.
  3. Use the turn-around. 7 × 8 is the same as 8 × 7, so the 144 facts up to 12 × 12 are really 78 facts.
  4. Few new facts at a time. 2–5 new facts a day, each with a picture or a trick.
  5. Bring mistakes back. A missed fact comes back a few minutes later, and again on the next days.
  6. Type the answers. The check is typed, so practise on a keyboard or a number pad, not only out loud.
  7. Start early. Year 3 or the autumn of Year 4 gives time for every fact without stress.

Questions

Is the multiplication tables check a pass or fail test?

No. There is no pass mark. Each child gets a score out of 25, and the school uses it to see who needs more help.

How many questions are in the MTC?

25 questions, with 6 seconds for each answer and a 3-second pause between questions. 3 practice questions come first.

Which times tables are in the Year 4 check?

The 2 to 12 times tables. Questions from the 6, 7, 8, 9 and 12 times tables come up more often.

When do children take the multiplication tables check?

In Year 4, during a three-week window in June. Your child's school sets the day.

Do children in Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland take it?

No. The multiplication tables check is for schools in England.
